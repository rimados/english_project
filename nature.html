Nature

Belovezhskaya Pushcha

One of the oldest primeval forests in Europe. It is home to the European bison (Zubr), the national symbol of Belarusian wildlife.

Braslav lakes

Belarus is often called the 'Blue-Eyed Country' because of its 11,000 lakes. Braslaw Lakes National Park is one of the most picturesque places with crystal-clear water.
